CrashGuard AI Privacy Notice (India — Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)

Last updated: April 2026

Data fiduciary: CrashGuard AI operates this application. Contact details should be published on your deployment’s website or in-app support channel.

What we process
• Location and motion/crash-related signals to detect emergencies and route help.
• Optional medical profile information you enter (blood group, allergies, conditions) to assist responders.
• Device identifiers needed for optional push notifications (Nearby SOS via Firebase Cloud Messaging).

Lawful basis
We collect and process personal data only after you provide explicit consent on first launch. You may withdraw consent by discontinuing use and deleting the app; optional cloud retention can be toggled in Settings.

Retention
• Cloud incident summaries: deleted automatically after 90 days unless you enable “extended retention” in Settings.
• Local encrypted copies on your device follow your device backup settings.

Sharing
Data may be transmitted to configured emergency contacts, responders, mesh peers, or your Supabase backend for sync — limited to providing assistance. We do not sell personal data.

Your rights (summary)
You may request access, correction, or erasure where applicable by contacting the fiduciary; regional-language copies of this notice are available in-app.

Nearby SOS (FCM)
If you opt in, Google Firebase may process a device token for topic-based alerts. You can disable this at any time in Settings.
